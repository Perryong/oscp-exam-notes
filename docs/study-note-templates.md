# Structured study notes

Open **Notes** in the relevant workspace:

- OSCP: choose **New OSCP box note**.
- OSAI / AI-300: choose **New AI-300 lab note**.
- **New note** still creates a blank note.

Rename the new note for the box or lab. Replace the editable prompts with your observations. Open **Details** to attach a machine/lab, tags or study week. Use the existing Screenshot control to add evidence. The editor saves and syncs through the same notebook workflow as other notes.

Creating a note from a machine or lab automatically uses the corresponding template. **Create lab note** in an AI lesson uses the AI-300 template and retains the lesson reference. Existing notes are never rewritten when a template changes.

## OSCP box template

Objectives and authorized scope; environment and prerequisites; enumeration; initial access; privilege escalation; commands and results; evidence; findings; lessons learned; references and next steps.

## AI-300 lab template

Objectives and authorized scope; environment and model version; prerequisites; numbered steps; prompts and commands; expected versus observed results; screenshots and evidence; repeated trials; findings; impact and limitations; mitigations and retest; lessons learned; references.

## Make notes reusable

Give steps, evidence and findings short IDs such as S-01, E-01 and F-01, and cross-reference them. Record exact inputs and commands in code blocks, expected and observed results, prerequisites, relevant versions and the reason for each next step. Keep unsuccessful trials and limitations. These records provide references for later authorized lab test planning; the templates do not execute commands or automate testing.
