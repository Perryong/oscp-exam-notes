OSCP Practice Ladder

Every box from the LainKusanagi OSCP-like list, ordered easiest to hardest, in weeks of at least 2 hours. Tick boxes as you root them, read a walkthrough inside the app, and keep notes, screenshots and commands for each week.

Where the walkthroughs come from

  • Walkthrough panel: opens inside this app. For HackTheBox boxes it shows IppSec's chapter list from his own video index, and each timestamp opens the video at that step. Every source can also open in a new tab.
  • 0xdf write-up: 0xdf's blog, the most widely cited written HackTheBox walkthroughs. He also covers VulnLab boxes that moved to HackTheBox.
  • OffSec official: every Proving Grounds box has an official walkthrough that unlocks on its machine page in the PG portal. That's the most reliable source for PG.
  • VulnHub entry: the PG Play boxes started on VulnHub, and each entry page lists community walkthroughs.
  • GitHub write-up: personal community repos (pika5164, Bsal13, AaronCaiii). They're useful but less vetted, so try the official one first. The list author also recommends Derron C's PG videos.
  • Pro Labs, HackSmarter, Hacker Blueprint and newer or active boxes have no well-known public write-ups. The OSCP-LK boxes come with write-ups from the list author.

Notes

  • Each week has its own notes. Add headings, lists, inline code and code blocks for commands. Add screenshots with the Image button, by pasting, or by dragging them in.
  • On claude.ai, notes save to your Claude account. On your own site, sign in with your email and password and notes sync to your Firebase project, so they're on every device. Until you sign in, they save in this browser only.
  • All notes lists every week you've written up. Search across notes, commands and box names, or export everything as one Markdown file.

How this was built

  • Difficulty comes from the community "LainKusanagi With Ratings" fork (PG's Intermediate is shown as Medium). Boxes it doesn't rate yet are estimated and marked est.
  • Time budget per box: Easy 1h, Medium 1.5h, Hard 2h, Very Hard 2.5h, +1h for AD chains and small networks, 3h per week on Pro Labs and ranges. If you finish early, pull the next week forward.
  • Inside each difficulty level, Linux comes first, then Windows, then Active Directory.
  • Left out: TryHackMe (the author no longer recommends it), Virtual Hacking Labs (list not maintained), AWS boxes (not in the exam) and the separate Red Teaming list.
